## Priority Issues

**[P0] No confirmation or undo on destructive delete actions**
Why it matters: hover-revealed .ekpi-remove sits near where mouse rests while scanning values; delete fires instantly with no recovery.
Fix: Add confirm() or toast-with-Undo before delete mutates state.
Suggested command: $impeccable harden

**[P0] Zero ARIA labels on icon-only controls**
Why it matters: icon-only buttons (delete/rename/info) have title= but no aria-label; screen readers announce bare "button". Compounds with 11 low-contrast and 7 undersized-text detector findings.
Fix: Add aria-label matching existing title text; fix flagged contrast/size values.
Suggested command: $impeccable audit

**[P1] Non-button interactive elements break keyboard access**
Why it matters: span/td onclick handlers (Oil Fund info, country toggle) have no tabindex/keydown handler, skipping keyboard users entirely.
Fix: Convert to button or add role="button" tabindex="0" + keydown handler.
Suggested command: $impeccable harden

**[P1] Low-contrast and undersized text across functional UI (detector-confirmed)**
Why it matters: white-on-blue at 3.6:1 and gray-on-black at 3.2:1 both fail WCAG AA 4.5:1; functional labels at 9.36-10.88px are hard to read in a data-dense interface.
Fix: Raise text to >=12px, correct color pairs to meet 4.5:1.
Suggested command: $impeccable typeset

**[P2] Error messages leak backend implementation detail**
Why it matters: "Firestore error" shown to end users with no actionable next step.
Fix: Replace with plain-language messaging, keep technical detail in console.error only.
Suggested command: $impeccable clarify
